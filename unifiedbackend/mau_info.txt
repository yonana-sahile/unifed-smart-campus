SECTION 2 — SYSTEM ROLES AND ACCESS
================================================================================

The system supports the following user roles:

1. STUDENT — Courses, materials, exams, grades, transcripts, finance,
   clearance, AI assistant.
2. INSTRUCTOR — Course management, exam creation and push, grade entry,
   materials upload, Zoom classroom, AI assistant.
3. REGISTRAR — Transcript generation, certificate issuance, student records,
   MoE admissions.
4. DEPARTMENT_HEAD — Course outlines, evaluations, department oversight.
5. ADMIN — Full system access, user management, system settings.
6. DEAN — Academic oversight, faculty-level reporting.
7. AUDITOR — Read-only access to audit logs and reports.
8. LIBRARY_STAFF — Library resources, digital library management.
9. FINANCE_OFFICER — Payments, scholarships, cost-sharing, clearance.

Login Flow:
- Username + Password → POST /api/token/
- Returns: { "refresh": "...", "access": "...", "user": {...} }
- The token is stored in browser localStorage as "access_token".
- Sent on every API request as "Authorization: Bearer <token>".

SECTION 3 — STUDENT WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

3.1 ACADEMIC DASHBOARD
Shows: Degree progress, CGPA, completed credit hours, academic standing,
campus node, active alerts.
Progress: X/180 CH completed, percentage toward graduation.
Academic Standing: Good Standing / Warning / Probation / Dismissal.

3.2 COURSE REGISTRATION (Browse & Register)
Steps:
1. Log in to student dashboard.
2. Click "Browse & Register" in the sidebar.
3. Search for courses by code or title.
4. Click "Enroll" next to the desired course.
5. Confirm registration. The course appears in "My Courses."

Rules:
- Late registration incurs a penalty after the deadline.
- Course capacity limits enrollment; if full, enroll fails.
- Prerequisites must be satisfied before enrolling.
- Cost-sharing balance must be cleared before exam registration.
- Maximum course load: 18 credit hours without department head approval.
- Add/drop window: 2 weeks after classes begin.

3.3 COURSE MATERIALS
- Browse materials per enrolled course.
- File types: PDF, Video, Document, Slide.
- Organized by chapter/week.
- Download directly from the portal.

3.4 ZOOM CLASSROOM
- Live sessions shown with "LIVE" badge.
- Click "Join" to enter the session (uses join URL + passcode).
- Recordings posted after the session ends.
- Status values: UPCOMING, LIVE, COMPLETED, CANCELLED.

3.5 ONLINE EXAMINATIONS
- Exams displayed with "LIVE PUSHED" badge when active.
- "Start Examination" opens a full-screen modal with a live countdown timer.
- Auto-save is enabled for all answers.
- Submit via "Submit Exam" button.
- The exam attempt is recorded with the score and timestamp.

Exam submission flow:
POST /api/exam-attempts/ with:
{
  "exam": <exam_id>,
  "student": <student_user_id>,
  "answers": { "<question_id>": "<selected_option>", ... },
  "score": <computed_score>,
  "status": "SUBMITTED",
  "submitted_at": "ISO timestamp"
}

3.6 GRADES & ASSESSMENTS
Assessment structure (50/20/30):
- Continuous Assessment: 50% (quizzes, labs, assignments, projects)
- Midterm Exam: 20%
- Final Exam: 30%
- Total: 100%

Letter grades:
- A: 90–100 (grade point 4.0)
- B: 80–89 (grade point 3.0)
- C: 70–79 (grade point 2.0)
- D: 60–69 (grade point 1.0)
- F: < 60 (grade point 0.0)

3.7 OFFICIAL TRANSCRIPT
- Request from "Official Transcript" in the sidebar.
- Generated with a unique verification code.
- Requires approval from the registrar.
- Includes cumulative GPA and total credits.

3.8 FINANCE & TUITION
Payment methods: Telebirr, CBE Birr, Awash Bank, Bank Transfer.
Payment types: Tuition, Cost Sharing, Dormitory, ID Card, Registration.
Payment statuses: VERIFIED, PENDING, REJECTED.
View outstanding balance under "Finance & Tuition" in the portal.

3.9 DIGITAL CLEARANCE
Workflow:
1. Submit clearance request from the "Digital Clearance" portal.
2. Departmental verification in this order:
   a. Library — return books, clear overdue fines
   b. Department Head — return lab kits, equipment, submit capstone thesis
   c. Student Service & Proctor — return dorm inventory and keys
   d. Registrar & Finance — settle fees, request transcript
3. Once all departments approve, a cryptographically signed Exit Certificate
   with a verifiable QR code is generated instantly.

Clearance reasons: GRADUATION, END_OF_YEAR, WITHDRAWAL, TRANSFER.
Overall statuses: IN_PROGRESS, APPROVED, BLOCKED.
Timeline: 3–7 working days if all departments clear.

SECTION 4 — GRADING AND HONORS
================================================================================

4.1 Assessment Model (50/20/30)
- Continuous Assessment: 50%
- Midterm Exam: 20%
- Final Exam: 30%

4.2 Graduation Distinctions (based on CGPA)
- Very Great Distinction (Gold Medal): CGPA ≥ 3.75 (no grade below B)
- Great Distinction: CGPA 3.50 – 3.74
- Distinction: CGPA 3.00 – 3.49
- Pass: CGPA 2.00 – 2.99

4.3 Academic Probation
- Semester GPA < 1.75 triggers an academic warning.
- Continued low performance results in probation, then dismissal.

4.4 Grade Appeal
- Request within 10 days of grade posting.
- Instructor responds within 5 working days.
- Escalate to Department Head, then Dean if unresolved.

SECTION 5 — EXAMS AND ASSESSMENTS
================================================================================

5.1 MoE Higher Education Exit Exam (HEEE)
- Eligibility: All graduating final-year undergraduate students.
- Pass mark: 50% cumulative aggregate score.
- Format: 100 standardized Multiple Choice Questions, 3 hours.
- Blueprint: Prepared by the Ministry of Education, covering department core
  competencies.

Sample curriculum breakdown (Software Engineering):
- Architecture: 25%
- Algorithms: 20%
- Databases: 20%
- Networks & Security: 15%
- QA & Testing: 10%
- Project Ethics: 10%

5.2 University Midterm and Final Exams
- Created by instructors via the Exam Management portal.
- Question types: MCQ, True/False, Short Answer.
- Pushed live to students via the "Push Exam" action.
- Timer runs in real-time during the exam.
- Continuous auto-save during the exam.

5.3 Exam Attendance Rules
- Arrive 15 minutes before the exam starts.
- No phones or smart devices in the exam hall.
- Only permitted materials allowed (calculator for math, etc.).
- Missing final exam = 0 unless excused with documentation within 5 days.

SECTION 7 — AI ACADEMIC ASSISTANT
================================================================================

7.1 Purpose
The MAU AI Academic Assistant is a 24/7 bilingual (English/Amharic) chat
assistant that answers questions about:
- MoE Exit Exam
- Grading and honors
- Digital clearance workflow
- Campuses and facilities
- Course registration
- Fees and payments
- Student services (dorm, cafe, health center, library)
- Any other university policy

7.2 Technology
- Backend: Django + Google Gemini (gemini-flash-latest)
- Knowledge: Long-context prompting using this document
- Frontend: React chat widget, floating bottom-right on every page

7.3 Response Style
- Concise, factual, and helpful.
- Replies in the same language as the user (English or Amharic).
- Cites sources when answers come from the context.
- Suggests contacting the registrar when unsure.

7.4 Example Questions the AI Can Answer
- "How do I register for a course?"
- "What is the exit exam pass mark?"
- "How does the digital clearance workflow work?"
- "Explain the 50/20/30 grading system."
- "What are the graduation distinction levels?"
- "Where is the Tulu Awliya campus?"
- "What are the cafe hours?"
- "How do I pay my tuition?"
- "What is academic probation?"

SECTION 14 — POLICIES AND RULES
================================================================================

14.1 Attendance Policy
- Minimum 80% attendance per course to sit for final exam.
- Medical excuses submitted within 5 days.
- 3 unexcused absences = warning; 5 = academic penalty.

14.2 Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism = automatic F for the assignment.
- Repeated plagiarism = academic probation or dismissal.
- Exams are proctored; cheating = immediate disqualification.

14.3 Exam Rules
- Arrive 15 minutes before the exam starts.
- No phones or smart devices in the exam hall.
- Only permitted materials allowed (calculator for math, etc.).

14.4 Dormitory Rules
- Curfew: 10:00 PM (Sun–Thu), 11:00 PM (Fri–Sat).
- Visitors only in common areas, not rooms.
- Overnight guests require proctor approval.

14.5 Library Rules
- Silent zones enforced.
- Books can be borrowed for 14 days.
- Overdue fine: 5 birr per day per book.

14.6 Grade Appeal Process
1. Request a re-evaluation within 10 days of grade posting.
2. Instructor responds within 5 working days.
3. If unresolved, escalate to Department Head.
4. Final appeal to the Dean.

14.7 Student Code of Conduct
- Respect for all students, staff, and property.
- No harassment, discrimination, or violence.
- Violations result in disciplinary hearings.
